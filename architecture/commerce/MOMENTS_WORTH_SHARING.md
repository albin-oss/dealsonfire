# DOF Buyer Experience — MOMENTS_WORTH_SHARING

**Status:** For Founder approval (design only) · v1.0 · 2026-07-27
Word of mouth is the street's only growth engine by law (no ads, no referral mechanics — Release 1.1 spirit). This document designs the moments people *naturally retell*, and the zero-friction paths for retelling them. A shared moment must pass three tests: it happened (real event), it flatters the *maker* (not the platform), and sharing it required no incentive.

---

## 1. The retell inventory (what people actually say to friends)

| Moment | The sentence a friend hears | Share carrier |
|---|---|---|
| The workshop wait | "She posted about MY blanket's dye lot while she was making it" | the wait-window spark is shareable with the existing spark link — no new surface |
| The parcel photo | "Look — this is my parcel before it left her table" | photo in the timeline; share = the existing share idiom on the order's public-safe card (§2) |
| The honest stumble | "It was going to be late and they told me first — refund already offered" | retold verbally; needs no button (and should have none — engineering a share path onto a failure would be grotesque) |
| The biography | "My order page is basically the story of the thing" | biography share card (§2) |
| The discovery itself | "Found this knitter on DOF, look at her shop" | share-the-shop (built, Release 1.1) |
| The keystone | "The maker doesn't even get paid until it ships" | carried inside every other share as ambient copy — never its own campaign |

## 2. The one new surface: the biography share card
Sharing an order today would leak PII (address-adjacent, price-adjacent). The design: a **public-safe rendering** the buyer explicitly mints — the thing's photo, the maker's name and link, "made in March, promised by the 14th, arrived 2 days early," optionally one wait-window spark. No price, no buyer identity beyond what they add themselves, no address, revocable. It is the digital equivalent of showing a friend the thing *and pointing at the stall* — every share is a storefront visit seeded with proof of a kept promise.
**Gate:** new public surface → founder approval at its increment (C6+); privacy review mandatory; default is unshared (minting is a deliberate act).

## 3. Anti-mechanics (banned, permanently)
Referral rewards ("give €5, get €5") — turns friends into channels and poisons the retell. Share-prompts after payment — begging at the till. Auto-generated social images with platform branding dominant — the maker is the story (share cards are brand-dressed in the *merchant's* kit, DOF small in the corner). Share-count displays — popularity is ranking's foot in the door.

## 4. Why this beats a referral program
A referral program buys mentions; this design manufactures *evidence worth mentioning*. The retell inventory above is available to every shop from its first order, costs the platform nothing per share, and every path terminates at a storefront wearing a kept promise — the trust layer and the growth layer are the same object seen from two sides.
