# DOF Buyer Experience — BUYER_SIGNATURE_MOMENTS

**Status:** For Founder approval (design only) · v1.0 · 2026-07-27
The moments a buyer will *remember* and retell. Delight with restraint (Experience Director law): each moment is one deliberate touch, never confetti-by-default. Restated from UX-BIBLE §14 discipline: a signature moment must be (a) rare, (b) earned by a real event, (c) skippable without loss.

---

## SM-1 · "There's someone here" (first visit)
The moment the presence line + latest spark land together: the templated-store dread breaks and the page becomes a person. **The touch:** none — the *content is* the moment. Restraint is the craft: any animation here would smell like marketing.
**Retell line we're designing for:** "it's like their actual workshop, you can see what they made this week."

## SM-2 · "It's mine-ish" (add to cart, built in C1)
Possession before payment. **The touch (exists):** the button's instant flip to "In your cart — view". No drawer slide-outs, no fly-to-cart animation — the street stays calm.

## SM-3 · The letter (confirmation, ⧗C3/C5)
Where every other shop prints a receipt, DOF writes a short letter: what happens next, when, in whose hands. Merchant's promise line as the sign-off. **The touch:** the page composes like correspondence (reading rhythm, generous space) — the one place in commerce where slowing the buyer down is right.
**Retell line:** "the confirmation actually told me the knitter starts on it Tuesday."

## SM-4 · The kept promise (delivery, ⧗C6)
The timeline's final entry: "Delivered — 2 days ahead of the promise." The promise-kept fact is *witnessed by the buyer* at the moment of joy, and they know it joins the maker's permanent record (their yes/no promise-check is the witness signature). **The touch:** the timeline entry renders in positive tone — the only celebratory color on the whole journey, earned by the only event that deserves it.

## SM-5 · The honest stumble (exceptions, ⧗C5+)
The signature moment nobody designs: something went wrong and *the platform said so first*. Promise-at-risk → the merchant's proactive note (Recovery Journey, merchant-signed) → or the no-ship auto-refund arriving before the buyer complained. **The touch:** speed and plainness. This is the moment that creates evangelists, because it is the moment every other marketplace fumbles.
**Retell line:** "it was going to be late and they told me BEFORE I asked — refund was already offered."

## SM-6 · The return (second visit)
Home says the maker posted; the corner remembers; ⧗C5 the shop can say "you've bought here before." **The touch:** recognition without surveillance-smell — facts the buyer gave DOF on purpose (a follow, an order), never inferred taste.

---

## Anti-moments (explicitly banned)
Celebration on *payment* (money leaving is not the buyer's joy — arrival is) · gamified streaks/points · "rate your experience!" interstitials · any moment that triggers on a percentage, score, or algorithmic inference rather than a real, singular event.
