# DOF Merchant Experience — MERCHANT_EMOTIONAL_MODEL

**Status:** For Founder approval (design only; everything approved stays frozen) · v1.0 · 2026-07-27
**Scope law:** composition only — every mechanism here renders existing architecture (momentum, pulse, sparks, promise record, Moment Ledger, trust ladder). No new capabilities.

## 1. What keeps a maker making (the psychology, condensed)

From the study set (bakers, roasters, woodworkers, jewelers, booksellers, market vendors), four fuels and four poisons:

**Fuels:** *finished things* (the loaf exists; craft has daily proof) · *being seen* (a regular saying "smells amazing" outweighs a spreadsheet) · *proprietorship* (the stall is MINE — my name, my rules, my corner) · *tomorrow's one thing* (a clear next task that is craft, not admin).

**Poisons:** *silence* (working into a void) · *admin creep* (the moment bookkeeping outweighs making) · *performance demand* (posting because an algorithm punishes silence — the Etsy/Instagram burnout engine) · *comparison* (dashboards that rank you against invisible others).

**The model in one line:** DOF must deliver the four fuels from real events and structurally refuse the four poisons — most of which incumbents cannot refuse, because their business models *are* the poisons (Etsy sells attention → must manufacture performance demand; Shopify sells software seats → must feel like software; Amazon sells logistics → sellers are inputs).

## 2. The counterfeit table (what we refuse, and what serves the same need honestly)

| Real need | Counterfeit (banned) | DOF's honest mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Momentum | streaks, "don't break the chain!" | the pulse: real facts in sentences ("2 new people followed this week — your words are working") |
| Being seen | vanity dashboards, follower graphs | "People saw this" moments + fires/follows shown as people, not curves |
| Progress | levels, badges, seller tiers | **the record**: promise-kept counts, the trust ladder (real capability gates), the shelf itself |
| Urgency | countdown nags ("post now!") | ONE sanctioned urgency: real external deadlines (disputes) — everything else waits calmly |
| Guidance | notification barrages | the mentor's ONE next thing, with its evidence, that never repeats itself (built law) |
| Return-tomorrow | daily-login rewards | tomorrow actually contains something: today's fires, a buyer waiting, one clear task |

## 3. The emotional arc: idea → hundredth order

- **"I have an idea"** → *Ignite*: the doors-open feeling in four minutes. Emotion: **it's real** — the live URL is a place, not a config.
- **First follower / first fire** → emotion: **witnessed**. A person, not a number ("someone new follows your store").
- **First sale (C5)** → **the moment** — the strongest emotion the platform will ever deliver (SIGNATURE_MOMENTS §2). Design law: the ceremony belongs to the *merchant's craft*, not to DOF's success.
- **First kept promise (C6)** → emotion: **proven**. "Your first promise, kept — it's on your record now." The record starts compounding; pride shifts from *having a store* to *having a reputation*.
- **First return buyer (C5+)** → emotion: **regularhood from the other side of the stall** — the single strongest retention emotion for makers ("someone came BACK").
- **Hundredth order** → emotion: **craftsman's quiet**. Scale must not convert the workshop into a console — the same one-thing rhythm, now backed by a longer record (LONG_TERM_RELATIONSHIP.md).

## 4. The inversion nobody else has: writing to people who are waiting

On every other platform, merchant content is marketing shouted at strangers. On DOF, once the Workshop Wait ships (approved), a spark lands in the timelines of **buyers who already paid and are waiting for this maker's hands**. The audience line evolves ("3 people are waiting on orders — they'll see this too"): posting stops being performance and becomes *hospitality*. This single fact dissolves the content-pressure poison at its root — the maker writes because someone real is waiting in the workshop doorway, not because reach decays. Amazon cannot build this (no maker voice); Etsy could only fake it (convo spam); Shopify has no fabric for it.

## 5. The dignity clauses

- **Silence is never punished.** No reach decay, no "your shop is slipping" — the street is chronological; a quiet week costs nothing but quiet. The presence line's stale-fallback (opened-date) protects merchants from their own quiet spells on the buyer side too.
- **Numbers never shame.** Zero states speak as invitations (built empty-state law); comparisons to other shops do not exist anywhere in the workspace.
- **The record heals by behavior** (confidence system §4) — a bad month is recoverable by work, never by payment.
- **The merchant is never the product.** No engagement KPIs drive any workspace surface; the only "engagement" DOF wants is shipped promises.
