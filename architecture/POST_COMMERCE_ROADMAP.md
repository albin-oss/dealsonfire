# POST-COMMERCE ROADMAP

**Status:** RATIFIED by Founder directive of 2026-08-08 · supersedes all prior forward sequences (`ROADMAP.md` Stream A/B, `IMPLEMENTATION-ROADMAP-v1.0` R2–R6 ordering, `commerce/IMPLEMENTATION_ROADMAP.md` post-C11 rows) as the operative direction. **Basis:** `architecture/POST_COMMERCE_STRATEGIC_REVIEW.md` (accepted) and the C11 closure evidence (`architecture/commerce/C11_PRODUCTION_CERTIFICATION.md`, GO).

## The accepted diagnosis

The Commerce Program (C1–C11) is complete and externally certified. Transaction correctness is no longer the constraint. The platform must now become **launchable, observable, discoverable, and capable of generating demand** — in that order. A binding corollary, accepted as principle: **no AI is built before DOF has real behavioral signals worth learning from.**

## The ratified sequence

| Phase | Program | Purpose | Exit condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Launch Foundations** (C12, redefined) | Make DOF safe and honest enough to put in front of real strangers | Every letter delivers; security state survives restarts; a buyer is a person, not a cookie; abuse has an intake and enforcement path; legal surfaces exist behind Founder/counsel gates; recovery is proven, not configured. Plan: `C12_LAUNCH_FOUNDATIONS_PROGRAM_PLAN.md` |
| **2** | **The Living Street** — Discovery & Demand | Attention: earn it, observe it, reward it | Buyers find things by intent, wandering, scent, and from the outside web; every engagement signal improves the street; merchants see their first honest demand receipts |
| **3** | **Merchant Evidence / Growth Intelligence** | Report the demand Phase 2 creates and measures | Merchants understand performance in sentences backed by real data; the workspace's mockup modules become true or are removed |
| **4** | **Trust & Reputation** | Verified-purchase reputation and scaled safety, once volume exists to make them honest | Buyer trust at the decision point rests on evidence, not merchant self-description |
| **5** | **AI capabilities** | Intelligence behind the long-prepared ports, fed by Phases 2–4 signals | AI that improves the marketplace (semantic discovery, photo-first authoring, companion sentences) — never demos |

## Binding rules of the sequence

1. **No phase begins without Founder authorization of its program plan.** Increment gates within phases follow the commerce-campaign discipline (hostile review at the seams, honest copy, sweep-then-tag release law).
2. **No silent reintroduction of deferred work.** Anything excluded by RELEASE-001 X-1…X-14, the strategic review's Part 12, or a phase's non-goals re-enters only by explicit Founder decision — never because an old roadmap lists it.
3. **Phase boundaries are ownership boundaries.** C12 must not build Living Street capabilities (see the prohibition list in the C12 plan §7); the Living Street must not build analytics reporting (Phase 3) or reputation (Phase 4).
4. **The AI ordering principle is constitutional for this roadmap:** Phases 2–4 create the signals; Phase 5 consumes them. Ports stay warm; models wait.
5. **Public launch is a milestone inside this roadmap, not a phase.** The heavyweight formal gates (pen test, load pass, DR at production scale, accessibility audit — RELEASE-001 G-3…G-11) attach to the public-launch decision at the end of Phase 2, on a street worth opening. Phase 1 closes only the stranger-safety subset.
6. **Founder decisions register.** Three decision areas (market/legal identity, growth-values boundary, brand identity) are recorded in the C12 plan §9 and must be resolved before the public-launch milestone — none blocks Phase 1 engineering from starting.
